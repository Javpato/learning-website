Exercise 2 — Representing transformations in the Clapeyron diagram

Statement

Consider one mole of ideal gas with constant molar heat capacities $C_p$ and $C_v$, undergoing the following sequence of transformations:

  • $A \Rightarrow B$: quasi-static isochoric compression with $P_B = 2P_A$;
  • $B \Rightarrow C$: quasi-static isobaric expansion with $V_C = 2V_A$;
  • $C \Rightarrow D$: quasi-static isothermal expansion with $V_D = 3V_A$;
  • $D \Rightarrow E$: reversible adiabatic expansion with $V_E = (4/3)^{1/\gamma}\,V_D$, where $\gamma$ is the adiabatic index.

Questions

  1. Express $P_E$ as a function of $P_A$.
  2. Draw these transformations on a Clapeyron diagram $(P, V)$.
  3. For each of the transformations $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DE$, give the expression of the work received, the heat received, and the change in internal energy. Deduce $\Delta U_{ABCDE}$.
  4. Give the work and heat received, as well as the change in internal energy, during the direct transformation $AE$, as functions of $T_A$ and $T_E$.
  5. Without any computation, compare graphically $W_{ABCDE}$ with $W_{AE}$, then $\Delta U_{ABCDE}$ with $\Delta U_{AE}$. Comment.

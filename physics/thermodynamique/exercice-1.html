Exercise 1 — Computing the work and heat received

Statement

Consider two moles of oxygen, assumed to be an ideal gas, taken from an initial state $A$ to a final state $B$ by three distinct reversible transformations represented in a Clapeyron diagram $(P, V)$.

We are given $P_B = 3P_A$ and $T_B = T_A$.

The three possible paths are:

  • $AMB$: isothermal transformation;
  • $ACB$: isochoric then isobaric transformation;
  • $ANB$: transformation represented by a straight line.

Questions

  1. State the equation of the curve representing the isotherm $AMB$.
  2. Determine and compare, as functions of $R$ and $T_A$, the work and the amount of heat involved in each of the transformations. Justify each answer.

Numerical data: $T_A = 300\ \text{K}$, $R = 8.32\ \text{J}\cdot\text{mol}^{-1}\cdot\text{K}^{-1}$, $n = 2$.

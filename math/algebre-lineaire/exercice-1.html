Exercise 1 — Trace and determinant of transposition

Statement

Let $n \ge 2$. Consider the map

$\varphi \colon M_n(\mathbb{R}) \to M_n(\mathbb{R}), \qquad \varphi(M) = M^{T}.$

We write $\mathrm{Sym}(n)$ for the set of symmetric matrices ($S^{T} = S$) and $\mathrm{Skew}(n)$ for the set of skew-symmetric matrices ($K^{T} = -K$).

Questions

  1. Show that $\varphi$ is a linear map.
  2. Show that $\varphi^2 = \mathrm{id}$. What does this tell us about the possible eigenvalues of $\varphi$?
  3. Show that $M_n(\mathbb{R}) = \mathrm{Sym}(n) \oplus \mathrm{Skew}(n)$ and give the dimensions of the two subspaces.
  4. Deduce the eigenspaces of $\varphi$ and show that $\varphi$ is diagonalizable.
  5. Compute $\operatorname{tr}(\varphi)$ and $\det(\varphi)$.
  6. Check the results in the concrete case $n = 2$.

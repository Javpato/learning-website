Exercise 2 — Nilpotency of differentiation on $\mathbb{R}_n[X]$

Statement

Let $n \ge 1$. We write $\mathbb{R}_n[X]$ for the space of real polynomials of degree at most $n$, equipped with the canonical basis $(1, X, X^2, \dots, X^n)$. Consider the differentiation operator

$D \colon \mathbb{R}_n[X] \to \mathbb{R}_n[X], \qquad D(p) = p'.$

Questions

  1. Show that $D$ is a linear map.
  2. Write the matrix of $D$ in the canonical basis (make the case $n = 3$ explicit).
  3. Show that $D^{n+1} = 0$ and that $D$ is nilpotent of order exactly $n + 1$.
  4. Determine the eigenvalues of $D$.
  5. Deduce that $D$ is not diagonalizable.

Exercise 1

Transposition: trace and determinant

The operator φ(M) = Mᵀ on Mₙ(ℝ) is an involution. We decompose the space into symmetric and skew-symmetric parts, deduce that φ is diagonalizable, then compute tr(φ) = n and det(φ) = (−1)n(n−1)/2.

Exercise 2

Differentiation on ℝₙ[X]: nilpotency

The differentiation operator D(p) = p′ drops the degree by one at each application. We write its matrix in the canonical basis, show Dⁿ⁺¹ = 0 with order exactly n + 1, and deduce that D is not diagonalizable.

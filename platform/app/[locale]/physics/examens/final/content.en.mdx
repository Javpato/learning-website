<ExamProblem title="Problem A — Nonuniformly charged sphere" points={10}>

A sphere of radius $R$ carries $\rho(r)=\rho_0(1-r/R)$. Find the total
charge, the field everywhere, the potential with $V(\infty)=0$, and the
radius at which the inner field is maximal.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé (worked solution) and indicative barème (mark scheme)">

$$
Q(r)=4\pi\rho_0\left(\frac{r^3}{3}-\frac{r^4}{4R}\right),
\qquad
Q(R)=\frac{\pi\rho_0R^3}{3}.
$$

Gauss's theorem gives

$$
E_{\rm in}=\frac{\rho_0}{\varepsilon_0}
\left(\frac r3-\frac{r^2}{4R}\right),\qquad
E_{\rm out}=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{12\varepsilon_0r^2}.
$$

After integration and matching at $R$,

$$
V_{\rm out}=\frac{\rho_0R^3}{12\varepsilon_0r},
\qquad
V_{\rm in}=\frac{\rho_0}{12\varepsilon_0}
\left(2R^2-2r^2+\frac{r^3}{R}\right).
$$

$dE_{\rm in}/dr=0$ gives $r=2R/3$. Barème: charge 2 pts, fields 3 pts,
potentials 3 pts, matching conditions and maximum 2 pts.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

<ExamProblem title="Problem B — Loop and magnetic dipole" points={8}>

Establish the axial field of a loop of radius $R$ carrying current $I$,
identify its far-field moment, then calculate the energy and force between
two small loops aligned along their axis and separated by a distance $z$.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé and indicative barème">

$$
B_z(z)=\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}
\sim\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2m_1}{z^3},
\qquad m_1=I\pi R^2.
$$

For an aligned second moment $m_2$,

$$
U=-m_2B_1=-\frac{\mu_0m_1m_2}{2\pi z^3},
\qquad
F_z=-\frac{3\mu_0m_1m_2}{2\pi z^4}.
$$

Barème: exact field 3 pts, limit and identification 2 pts, energy and
signed force 3 pts.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

<ExamProblem title="Problem C — Quadrupolar molecule" points={12}>

$+q$ is at $z=\pm a$, and $-2q$ is at the origin.

1. Show that the monopole and dipole vanish.
2. For $z>a$, find the exact axial potential and its leading term.
3. Deduce the field, decay law, and energy with a test charge $Q$.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé and indicative barème">

$Q_{\rm tot}=0$ and $p_z=qa+q(-a)-2q(0)=0$. On the axis,

$$
V(z)=k_eq\left(\frac1{z-a}+\frac1{z+a}-\frac2z\right)
\sim\frac{2k_eqa^2}{z^3}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
E_z=-\frac{dV}{dz}\sim\frac{6k_eqa^2}{z^4},
\qquad
U_Q\sim\frac{2k_eQqa^2}{z^3}.
$$

The quadrupole potential decays as $r^{-3}$ and its field as $r^{-4}$,
faster than the monopole and dipole terms. Barème: moments 3 pts, exact
expression 3 pts, expansion 3 pts, field/interaction/interpretation 3 pts.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

<ExamProblem title="Problem D — Anisotropically polarizable molecule" points={10}>

A molecule with no permanent dipole has $\alpha_\parallel$ along its axis
and $\alpha_\perp$ transverse to it. It lies in the field of an ion $Q$;
its axis makes an angle $\theta$ with the radial direction. Find the
energy, stable orientation, radial force, and two short-distance
limitations of the model.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé and indicative barème">

With
$\alpha_{\rm eff}(\theta)=\alpha_\perp\sin^2\theta+
\alpha_\parallel\cos^2\theta$ and $E=k_e|Q|/r^2$,

$$
U=-\frac12\alpha_{\rm eff}(\theta)
\frac{k_e^2Q^2}{r^4},
\qquad
F_r=-2\alpha_{\rm eff}(\theta)\frac{k_e^2Q^2}{r^5}.
$$

The stable orientation maximizes $\alpha_{\rm eff}$: the axis is radial if
$\alpha_\parallel>\alpha_\perp$ and perpendicular in the opposite case.
At short distances, the point-like, linear model notably omits charge
penetration, nonlinear polarization, exchange repulsion, quantum
dispersion, and electronic reorganization.

Barème: tensor/energy 3 pts, orientation 2 pts, force 2 pts, physically
argued limitations 3 pts.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

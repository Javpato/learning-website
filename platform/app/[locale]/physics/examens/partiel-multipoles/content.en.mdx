<ExamProblem title="Exercise 1 — Molecular multipoles" points={10}>

Charges $+q$ are at $(\pm a,0,0)$, and charges $-q$ are at
$(0,\pm b,0)$.

1. Calculate the total charge and dipole.
2. Calculate the diagonal components of the quadrupole using
   $Q_{ij}=\sum_nq_n(3x_{n,i}x_{n,j}-r_n^2\delta_{ij})$.
3. Give the leading far-field term and discuss $a=b$.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé and indicative barème (mark scheme)">

**Monopole and dipole (3 pts).** $Q=2q-2q=0$. Each pair is symmetric under
inversion, so $\mathbf p=\mathbf0$.

**Quadrupole (5 pts).** The off-diagonal components vanish, and

$$
Q_{xx}=4qa^2+2qb^2,\qquad
Q_{yy}=-2qa^2-4qb^2,\qquad
Q_{zz}=-2qa^2+2qb^2.
$$

The trace is indeed zero. The leading potential is

$$
V_2(\mathbf r)=\frac1{4\pi\varepsilon_0}
\frac1{2r^3}\sum_{ij}Q_{ij}\hat r_i\hat r_j.
$$

**Case $a=b$ (2 pts).** This gives
$Q_{xx}=6qa^2$, $Q_{yy}=-6qa^2$, $Q_{zz}=0$: the quadrupole remains
nonzero and distinguishes the axes carrying the positive and negative
charges.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

<ExamProblem title="Exercise 2 — Ion–molecule interaction" points={10}>

An ion $Q$ lies on the axis of a molecule with permanent dipole $p$ and
isotropic polarizability $\alpha$. The angle between $\mathbf p$ and the
ion–molecule axis is $\theta$.

1. Write the permanent and induced energies.
2. Find the optimal attractive orientation and the radial force.
3. Find the distance at which the two energy contributions have the same
   magnitude.

<Collapsible title="Corrigé and indicative barème">

With $k_e=1/(4\pi\varepsilon_0)$,

$$
U(r,\theta)=-\frac{k_eQp\cos\theta}{r^2}
-\frac12\alpha\frac{k_e^2Q^2}{r^4}.
$$

The orientation selects the sign of $\cos\theta$ so that
$Qp\cos\theta>0$. In this orientation,

$$
F_r=-\frac{2k_e|Q|p}{r^3}
-\frac{2\alpha k_e^2Q^2}{r^5}.
$$

Equating the magnitudes
$k_e|Q|p/r^2=\alpha k_e^2Q^2/(2r^4)$ gives

$$
r_c^2=\frac{\alpha k_e|Q|}{2p}.
$$

Barème: energy and factor $1/2$ 4 pts, orientation/force 3 pts, crossover
and dimensions 3 pts.

</Collapsible>

</ExamProblem>

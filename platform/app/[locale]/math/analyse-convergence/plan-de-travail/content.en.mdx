# 14-week study plan

A suggested route, **purely indicative**. Nothing here opens or closes
anything: every lesson, every TD (tutorial sheet), and every paper is
accessible right now, in whatever order you choose. If you arrive partway
through the semester, start with the week that matches your course, then go
back to what you are missing.

The principle behind this plan is that we retain what we retrieve from
memory, not what we reread. Each week therefore includes **spaced recall**
from an earlier week, to be done without notes, in ten minutes.

## How to revise (ten minutes worth an hour)

1. **Close everything.** Write from memory the list of reference series and
   their convergence conditions. Then check against the [formula sheet](../formulaire/).
2. **Reconstruct the counterexamples**, not just their names: a sequence
   that tends to zero without its series converging, pointwise convergence
   that is not uniform, uniform convergence whose limit is not differentiable,
   a uniformly convergent series that is not normally convergent.
3. **Redo a question you have already answered successfully**, cold, one week
   later. It is the exercise with the greatest payoff.
4. **Write out a complete hypothesis passport** for an interchange, without
   a model in front of you.

## After an assessment: classify your errors

Do not count the points lost; count the **types** of error — the type tells
you what to redo.

| Label | What it means | The remediation exercise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C** concept | confusing two notions (pointwise/uniform, necessary/sufficient) | reread the definition, then produce the counterexample that separates them |
| **H** hypothesis | theorem applied without checking its conditions | rewrite the question with the four-line passport |
| **M** method | wrong test chosen, or an inconclusive test used anyway | redo the exercise, justifying the choice of test before calculating |
| **A** algebra | isolated calculation error | redo only the calculation, without redoing the exercise |
| **L** logic | converse mistaken for implication, quantifiers reversed | rewrite the statement with the quantifiers in the correct order |
| **R** writing | correct result, missing justification | rewrite the answer using the models from the formula sheet |
| **T** time | question not attempted | redo the paper, answering only the first questions of each exercise |

The same error repeated three times under the same label is not a careless
mistake: it is the topic of your next study session.

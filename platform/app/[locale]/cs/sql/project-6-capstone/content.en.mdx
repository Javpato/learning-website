### 2 · Revenue by category

A line's revenue is `quantity * unit_price`. Sum it per category.

<SqlExercise
  task="Show each category name and its total revenue (rounded to 2 decimals), highest first. Join order_items → products → categories."
  starter={`SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue\nFROM order_items oi\nJOIN products p   ON oi.product_id = p.product_id\nJOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id\nGROUP BY cat.name\nORDER BY `}
  expected="SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue FROM order_items oi JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.product_id JOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id GROUP BY cat.name ORDER BY revenue DESC;"
  hint="Join the three tables, SUM(quantity*unit_price), GROUP BY category name, ORDER BY revenue DESC."
/>

### 3 · Top customers by spend

<SqlExercise
  task="Show the 3 biggest-spending customers: full name and total spend (rounded). Join customers → orders → order_items."
  starter={`SELECT c.first_name || ' ' || c.last_name AS customer,\n       ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS spend\nFROM customers c\nJOIN orders o      ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id\nJOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id\nGROUP BY c.customer_id\nORDER BY spend DESC\nLIMIT `}
  expected="SELECT c.first_name || ' ' || c.last_name AS customer, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS spend FROM customers c JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id GROUP BY c.customer_id ORDER BY spend DESC LIMIT 3;"
  hint="Group by customer, SUM the line revenue, ORDER BY spend DESC, LIMIT 3."
/>

### 4 · Average order value

First total each order, then average those totals.

<SqlExercise
  task="Compute the average order value (rounded to 2 decimals): total each order, then average the per-order totals."
  starter={`SELECT ROUND(AVG(order_total), 2) AS avg_order_value\nFROM (\n  SELECT o.order_id, SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS order_total\n  FROM orders o\n  JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id\n  GROUP BY o.order_id\n);`}
  expected="SELECT ROUND(AVG(order_total), 2) FROM (SELECT o.order_id, SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS order_total FROM orders o JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id GROUP BY o.order_id);"
  hint="The inner query gives one total per order; the outer query averages them."
/>

### 5 · Monthly order volume

<SqlExercise
  task="Show how many orders were placed in each month, as 'YYYY-MM', ordered chronologically."
  starter={`SELECT STRFTIME('%Y-%m', order_date) AS month, COUNT(*) AS n\nFROM orders\nGROUP BY month\nORDER BY `}

/>

### 6 · High-revenue categories (HAVING)

<SqlExercise
  task="List only the categories whose total revenue exceeds 300. Show the category name and its revenue (rounded)."
  starter={`SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue\nFROM order_items oi\nJOIN products p   ON oi.product_id = p.product_id\nJOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id\nGROUP BY cat.name\nHAVING `}
  expected="SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue FROM order_items oi JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.product_id JOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id GROUP BY cat.name HAVING SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) > 300;"
  hint="Same join + group as panel 2, but add HAVING SUM(quantity*unit_price) > 300."
/>

### 7 · The integrative finale — revenue by category, excluding cancelled orders

This one uses **all four tables, a `WHERE` filter, a `JOIN` chain, aggregation, and
ordering** at once. Notice how excluding cancelled orders reshuffles the ranking from
panel 2.

<SqlExercise
  task="Total revenue per category, EXCLUDING cancelled orders, rounded and ordered highest first. Join customers' orders → order_items → products → categories and filter out status 'cancelled'."
  starter={`SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue\nFROM orders o\nJOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id\nJOIN products p     ON oi.product_id = p.product_id\nJOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id\nWHERE o.status <> 'cancelled'\nGROUP BY cat.name\nORDER BY `}
  expected="SELECT cat.name, ROUND(SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 2) AS revenue FROM orders o JOIN order_items oi ON o.order_id = oi.order_id JOIN products p ON oi.product_id = p.product_id JOIN categories cat ON p.category_id = cat.category_id WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled' GROUP BY cat.name ORDER BY revenue DESC;"
  hint="Add WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled' before grouping — the two cancelled orders drop two Headset sales, so Electronics falls in the ranking."
/>

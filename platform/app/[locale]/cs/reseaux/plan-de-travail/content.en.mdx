# Study plan — six weeks, from binary to TCP

<ProvenanceBadge provenance="cours" />

A **suggested** path, never an imposed one: everything stays open, in
whatever order you like, and nothing here records anything. The pace
assumes about 4 to 5 hours a week; every week ends with exercises —
you learn by solving, the lessons are there to unblock you.

## Week 1 — The journey of a message

- [Toolbox](../00-boite-a-outils/): 30 minutes to check
  binary, hex and units — half the module's marks ride on them.
- [Lesson 01](../01-reseaux-et-commutation/): circuits, messages,
  packets; datagram versus virtual circuit.
- [Lesson 02](../02-delais-et-debits/): transmission, propagation,
  chronograms, effective throughput.
- **Exercises**: [TD 4](../td-4/) (tutorial sheet), exercise 1 (optimal
  packet size) — and the mini-exercises of both lessons.

## Week 2 — The layers, and the bottom of the stack

- [Lesson 03](../03-couches-et-encapsulation/): services, protocols,
  encapsulation, OSI and TCP/IP.
- [Lesson 04](../04-transmission-physique/): NRZ, Manchester, valence,
  Shannon.
- **Exercises**: [TD 1](../td-1/), exercises 1 to 4.

## Week 3 — Making a link reliable, sharing a cable

- [Lesson 05](../05-detection-des-erreurs/): parity, Hamming, CRC.
- [Lesson 06](../06-liaison-de-donnees/): ARQ, windows, HDLC, REJ/SREJ.
- [Lesson 07](../07-reseaux-locaux/): CSMA/CD, Ethernet, token.
- **Exercises**: [TD 1](../td-1/) exercises 5 to 7, [TD 2](../td-2/)
  in full, [TD 3](../td-3/) in full.
- **Milestone**: the [1-hour midterm](../examens/cc/) under real
  conditions — 60 minutes, without documents the first time.

## Week 4 — IP: addresses and datagrams

- [Lesson 08](../08-adressage-ip/): the carve-up into subnets — the
  most profitable lesson of the module (8 points at every partiel
  (midterm)).
- [Lesson 09](../09-datagramme-ip/): header, fragmentation, ARP, ICMP.
- **Exercises**: [TD 5](../td-5/) — long: start with 1, 4, 6, 9, 10,
  keep the ARP frames (12) and traceroute (13) for the end.

## Week 5 — Routing, and the mock midterm

- [Lesson 10](../10-routage/): tables, distance vectors, count to
  infinity, Dijkstra.
- **Exercises**: [TD 4](../td-4/) exercises 2 to 4, [TD 5](../td-5/)
  exercise 11.
- **Milestone**: the [2-hour midterm](../examens/partiel/) under real
  conditions. Mark yourself with the scheme, note your mistakes in the
  margin of the [formula sheet](../formulaire/) (section 10: you will
  very likely find yours there).

## Week 6 — Transport, and the exam

- [Lesson 11](../11-transport-et-tcp/): ports, UDP, SEQ/ACK, the
  three-way handshake.
- [Lesson 12](../12-fenetres-et-fiabilite-tcp/): windows, RTT, RTO.
- [Lesson 13](../13-controle-de-congestion/): slow start, AIMD, Tahoe
  and Reno.
- **Exercises**: [TD 6](../td-6/) in full; the [sockets lab](../td-7/)
  if you want to touch the code (it lights up everything else).
- **Milestone**: the [mock final exam](../examens/final/).

## The night before the exam

Reread the [formula sheet](../formulaire/) from end to end (20 minutes),
pausing on sections 10 and 11 — the mistakes that cost marks and the
expected sentences. Then sleep: a chronogram is drawn better rested.

## If you only have two weeks

Go where the marks are: lesson 08 + TD 5 (addressing), lesson 10 + TD 4
(routing), lesson 02 (chronograms), then the mock midterm. Revise the
MCQs with each lesson's “Quick checks” — they reuse the real questions
from the past papers.
